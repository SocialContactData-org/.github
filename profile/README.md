## Welcome to the Social Contact Data community 👋

This is the community space for [socialcontactdata.org](https://socialcontactdata.org), an open collection of social contact survey data, shared on Zenodo for research on infectious diseases and beyond.

### 💬 Join the conversation

Have a question about the data, an idea, or work to share?
**👉 [Go to our Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/SocialContactData-org/discussions)****

- ❓ **Q&A:** ask about the datasets, how to use or cite them
- 💡 **Ideas:** suggest new data or improvements
- 📣 **Announcements:** news from the team

Anyone with a GitHub account can post. Before asking, try searching: your question may already have an answer!

### 🔗 Useful links

- 🌐 Website: [socialcontactdata.org](https://socialcontactdata.org)
- 📊 Datasets: available on Zenodo, linked from the website
